Guide: [Prof. Balamurugan Palaniappan](https://www.ieor.iitb.ac.in/balamurugan.palaniappan)

This work was done as part of the **paper review** in the course IE 643 Deep Learning - Theory and Practice. I reviewed the literature for the problem of **adapting semantic segmentation** models to a domain of labels, going through the works **ADVENT, Open Source Adaptation,** and **TACS**.

Replicated the results of TACS using the open-source code, after a detailed survey of the paper, and implemented a modification using a **post-training ensemble-based method** to improve the mean IoU percentage by 2-8% over different taxonomies (up to 25-30% increase in mean IoU percentage for the worst performing labels).
