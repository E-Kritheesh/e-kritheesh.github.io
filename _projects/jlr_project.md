Interned full-time at **Jaguar Land Rover TBSI** (place of posting was Bengaluru) from May to July 2023. I worked on **two** projects during this 8-week internship.

**Project 1:**

Analysed different implementations of **Space Vector PWM (SVPWM)** and developed an efficient Simulink model from scratch.
Replaced the in-built PWM Generator block with my implementation in an example DC-DC converter Simscape project and demonstrated the higher DC utilisation and better THD.

**Project 2:**

Configured the ADC module in the TI F28P65 processor (C2000 series) for software interrupts-based sampling.
Tested and showed the converted outputs using a DC power supply by varying the voltages to the ADC input pin.

**Hackathon:**

Clinched the **first** position in a 36-hour hackathon for interns on the topic *"Vehicle as a Tour Guide"* by proposing a feature to reduce **range anxiety** in trips, researching **battery consumption** and **charging time estimation**.
Participated in a team of five interns, and this feature was my individual contribution. Our team also developed a prototype of the tour assistant integrated into the dashboard in the car.
