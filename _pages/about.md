Hi! My name is [Kritheesh](https://e-kritheesh.github.io). I am a graduate charging software engineer at [Jaguar Land Rover](https://www.jlr.com/), based out in Bangalore, India. I completed my undergraduate major degree in [Electrical Engineering](https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/web/index.php) with honours and a minor in [Computer Science & Engineering](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/) from [IIT Bombay](https://www.iitb.ac.in/).

I am currently (mid 2025) interested in hardware security, computer architecture, and embedded systems programming (and all things electrical!).

I have worked with [Prof. Biswa](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~biswa/), exploring the offensive side of architecture attacks and creating stealthier and agile cache attacks. I was also a part of the research group [CASPER](https://casper-iitb.github.io/).

I have also worked with [Prof. Saravanan](https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/~sarva/) on validity proofs for Bitcoin header chain consensus rules using Nova.

In my free time, I solve puzzles, play badminton, foosball, learn new languages, and play the keyboard.
