This work was done in my part-time internship under [Prof. Milind Sohoni](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~sohoni/) (Dept. of CSE, IIT Bombay) during Summer 2022.
PoCRA is a joint effort by the Government of Maharashtra and IIT Bombay, aided by the World Bank.
I was part of a **multidisciplinary** project team focused on **water and energy modelling** to identify constraints, develop solutions, and implement extension programs to protect smallholder farmers.

I got acquainted with PostgreSQL, POSTGIS, and PyQGIS APIs, and integrated the **GIS-based model** to use groundwater recharge to determine initial head values.
I also learnt to use data structures in a real-world application project and version control using Git.
